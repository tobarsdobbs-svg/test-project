# Content Production System & SOPs

A repeatable, systematized process for producing educational content
autonomously on a weekly basis. Follow this system to maintain consistent
output without burnout.

## 2. Content Ideation System

### Where to Find Topics (Monthly Research SOP)

**Step 1: Audience Questions**
- Review comments on your YouTube videos (the questions people ask = content ideas)
- Review community forum posts (what are members struggling with?)
- Review email replies and DMs
- Check BiggerPockets forums for trending questions
- **Output**: List of 10–15 topic ideas from audience

**Step 2: Keyword Research**
- Tools: TubeBuddy, vidIQ (for YouTube), Ahrefs or Ubersuggest (for blog)
- Search for keywords with: high search volume + low-medium competition
- Focus on long-tail keywords (e.g., "how to analyze a rental property" vs. "real estate")
- **Output**: List of 10–15 keyword-driven topic ideas

**Step 3: Competitor Audit**
- Review top-performing videos from 5 competitor YouTube channels
- Sort by "most popular" — what topics get the most views?
- Don't copy — look for gaps. What are they NOT covering? What could be better?
- **Output**: List of 5–10 differentiated topic ideas

**Step 4: News & Trends**
- Monitor: BiggerPockets blog, PwC Emerging Trends, CNBC Real Estate, local MLS data
- Create content around interest rate changes, market shifts, new laws, tax changes
- Timely content gets outsized engagement
- **Output**: List of 3–5 timely topic ideas

**Step 5: Content Calendar Population**
- From the combined list (30–45 ideas), select 8–12 for the next month
- Assign to content slots (YouTube, blog, email)
- Balance: 40% beginner, 30% intermediate, 20% timely/news, 10% advanced
- **Output**: Populated monthly content calendar

### The Content Idea Bank

Maintain a running document or spreadsheet with the following columns:

| Topic | Source | Keyword | Search Volume | Priority | Status |
|-------|--------|---------|---------------|----------|--------|
| How to analyze a rental property | Audience Q | "rental property analysis" | 12,000/mo | High | Published |
| BRRRR strategy explained | Competitor gap | "BRRRR method" | 8,000/mo | High | Drafted |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Never sit down to create content without knowing what you're making.
The system produces the ideas; your job is to execute.

## 3. YouTube Video Production SOP

### Pre-Production (Monday)

**Step 1: Select Topic**
- Pull from content calendar
- Confirm keyword target

**Step 2: Write Outline**
Use this framework for every video:

```
HOOK (0:00–0:30)
  - Open with a bold statement, question, or result
  - "In this video, I'll show you [specific outcome]"
  - Pattern interrupt: Say something unexpected

CONTEXT (0:30–2:00)
  - Why this topic matters
  - Who this is for
  - What they'll learn (roadmap the video)

CONTENT (2:00–15:00)
  - Break into 3–5 clear sections
  - Each section: Concept → Example → Action step
  - Use screen share for numbers/spreadsheets
  - Use B-roll or diagrams for visual variety

CTA (15:00–16:00)
  - "If you want [deeper resource], grab my free [Lead Magnet]
    — link in the description"
  - "Subscribe if you want [outcome] every [frequency]"
  - "Drop a comment with [engagement prompt]"
```

**Step 3: Prepare Visuals**
- Spreadsheet demos: Pre-populate with example data
- Slides: Keep minimal — images > text
- B-roll list: What supplemental footage is needed?

### Production (Tuesday)

**Recording Setup Checklist:**
- [ ] Camera on, framing correct (head and shoulders, rule of thirds)
- [ ] Microphone on, levels tested
- [ ] Lighting: Key light on face, no harsh shadows
- [ ] Background: Clean, relevant (home office, bookshelf, property)
- [ ] Screen recording software ready (if screenshare needed)
- [ ] Outline visible (teleprompter or notes beside camera)
- [ ] Record a 10-second test clip — review audio/video quality

**Recording Tips:**
- Record the hook 2–3 times (pick the best one in editing)
- Speak in short paragraphs — pause between sections for easy editing
- Look at the camera lens, not the screen
- Energy should be 20% higher than normal conversation
- Mistakes are fine — you'll edit them out
- Aim for 1.5x your target length (a 15-min video needs ~22 min of raw footage)

### Post-Production (Wednesday)

**Editing SOP:**
1. Import footage into editor (DaVinci Resolve [free], Premiere Pro, or Final Cut)
2. Cut dead air, ums, and mistakes
3. Add jump cuts between sections for pacing
4. Insert B-roll, screen recordings, and graphics
5. Add lower-thirds (name, key terms)
6. Add intro/outro bumper (consistent branding)
7. Color correct and audio normalize
8. Export at 1080p minimum (4K preferred)
9. Create thumbnail (see below)

**Thumbnail Creation SOP:**
- Tool: Canva (free) or Photoshop
- Template: Your face (expressive) + 3–5 words of text + high-contrast colors
- Text: Summarize the video's promise (not the title — complement it)
- Size: 1280x720 pixels
- Maintain consistent brand colors and fonts across all thumbnails
- A/B test thumbnails with TubeBuddy

**Upload & Optimization:**
- Title: Primary keyword + benefit (under 60 characters)
- Description: First 2 lines = keyword + CTA to lead magnet. Include timestamps.
- Tags: 5–10 relevant tags (primary keyword + variations)
- Cards: Link to related videos at relevant moments
- End screen: Subscribe button + next video + lead magnet link
- Publish time: Test and optimize (common: Tue/Thu 8 AM or 12 PM ET)

## 4. Short-Form Content Production SOP

### Repurposing Long-Form to Short-Form

**Step 1: Identify Clip-Worthy Moments**
While editing the long-form video, timestamp moments that:
- Contain a surprising fact or bold claim
- Explain a concept in under 60 seconds
- Include a specific number or result
- Have high energy or emotional impact
- Answer a common question

**Step 2: Cut Clips**
- Length: 30–90 seconds (60 sec is the sweet spot)
- Format: Vertical (9:16) for Reels, TikTok, Shorts
- Add captions/subtitles (CapCut auto-captions or Rev.com)
- Add a hook text overlay in the first 2 seconds

**Step 3: Post Strategy**
- Post 1–2 clips per day across platforms
- Use platform-native features (TikTok trending sounds, IG stickers)
- Caption formula: Hook → Context → CTA
- CTA: "Follow for more" or "Link in bio for free [resource]"

## 5. Blog / SEO Article Production SOP

### Article Framework

```
TITLE: Include primary keyword. Under 60 characters.
  Format: "How to [Outcome]" or "[Number] [Things] About [Topic]"

META DESCRIPTION: 150–160 characters. Include keyword + benefit.

INTRODUCTION (100–200 words)
  - Hook: Surprising stat, question, or bold statement
  - Context: Why this matters
  - Preview: What the article covers

H2 SECTIONS (3–7 sections, 300–500 words each)
  - Each section = one subtopic or step
  - Include examples, data, or personal experience
  - Use bullet points and tables for scannability
  - Naturally include keyword variations

CONCLUSION (100–150 words)
  - Summarize key takeaways
  - CTA: Download lead magnet or watch related video

INTERNAL LINKS: Link to 2–3 related articles on your site
EXTERNAL LINKS: Link to 1–2 authoritative sources
IMAGES: 2–4 relevant images with alt text
LEAD MAGNET CTA: In-content box at the 30% and 70% scroll marks
```

### Publishing Checklist
- [ ] Article is 2,000+ words
- [ ] Primary keyword in title, first paragraph, and 2–3 H2s
- [ ] Meta description written
- [ ] Images have alt text
- [ ] Internal and external links included
- [ ] Lead magnet CTA embedded
- [ ] Article is formatted for mobile readability
- [ ] Social sharing image set (Open Graph)
- [ ] Published and indexed (submit to Google Search Console)

## 6. Email Newsletter Production SOP

### Weekly Newsletter Process

**Step 1: Choose One Insight (Monday)**
- What did you learn, notice, or do this week in your investing business?
- What question did a student or audience member ask?
- What market trend deserves commentary?
- What mistake should people avoid?

**Step 2: Draft (Thursday — 30 min)**
Use this structure:

```
Subject Line: [Curiosity-driven, specific, under 50 characters]
Preview Text: [Complements subject, adds context]

HOOK (2–3 sentences)
  Story, observation, or surprising fact.

LESSON (200–400 words)
  One idea. Be specific. Use examples and numbers.
  Short paragraphs. Conversational tone.

ACTION STEP (1–2 sentences)
  Tell them what to do with this information.

CTA (1 sentence)
  Link to video, blog, webinar, or course.

SIGN-OFF
  Consistent, personal.
```

**Step 3: Review & Schedule (Thursday — 15 min)**
- Read aloud for tone and flow
- Check all links work
- Schedule for Friday morning (8–10 AM in your audience's timezone)
