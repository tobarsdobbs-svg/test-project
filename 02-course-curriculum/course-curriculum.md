# Real Estate Investing Course Curriculum

This document contains three complete course outlines — one per audience tier.
Launch the **Beginner course first** (largest audience), then build upward.

---

## COURSE 1: "First Deal Blueprint" (Beginner)

**Price point**: $497–$997
**Format**: Self-paced video + downloadable worksheets + community access
**Length**: 8 modules, ~25 hours of video content
**Outcome**: Student identifies, analyzes, finances, and closes their first investment property

### Module 1: The Investor Mindset & Goal Setting
- Lesson 1.1: Why Real Estate Beats Other Asset Classes (Cash flow, appreciation, tax benefits, leverage, inflation hedge)
- Lesson 1.2: The 5 Wealth-Building Strategies in RE (Rental income, appreciation, mortgage paydown, tax advantages, forced equity)
- Lesson 1.3: Setting Your Investment Goals (Income replacement calculator, timeline planning)
- Lesson 1.4: Common Fears and How to Overcome Them (Risk mitigation frameworks)
- Lesson 1.5: Building Your Investor Identity (From consumer to investor mindset shift)
- **Worksheet**: Personal Investment Goal Planner
- **Action Item**: Define 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year investment targets

### Module 2: Understanding Real Estate Markets
- Lesson 2.1: Macro vs. Micro Market Analysis
- Lesson 2.2: The 7 Key Market Indicators (Population growth, job growth, income growth, rent-to-price ratio, vacancy rates, permit activity, days on market)
- Lesson 2.3: How to Research a Market from Your Laptop (Data sources: Census, BLS, Zillow, Redfin, Rentometer)
- Lesson 2.4: Local vs. Out-of-State Investing — Pros and Cons
- Lesson 2.5: Identifying Emerging Neighborhoods (Gentrification indicators, infrastructure investment signals)
- **Worksheet**: Market Scorecard Template
- **Action Item**: Score and rank 3 target markets

### Module 3: Property Types & Investment Strategies
- Lesson 3.1: Single-Family Rentals — The Best Starting Point
- Lesson 3.2: Small Multifamily (2–4 Units) & House Hacking
- Lesson 3.3: Fix-and-Flip Overview — Is It Right for You?
- Lesson 3.4: Wholesaling Explained — Earning Without Buying
- Lesson 3.5: Short-Term Rentals (Airbnb) — Opportunity and Risk
- Lesson 3.6: Introduction to the BRRRR Strategy
- **Worksheet**: Strategy Selection Matrix (match your capital, time, risk tolerance to a strategy)
- **Action Item**: Select your primary investment strategy

### Module 4: Finding Deals
- Lesson 4.1: The MLS — Working with Investor-Friendly Agents
- Lesson 4.2: Off-Market Deal Sources (Direct mail, driving for dollars, networking, wholesalers)
- Lesson 4.3: Online Deal Sources (Auction sites, Zillow, Redfin, Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace)
- Lesson 4.4: Building a Deal Pipeline (Consistent lead flow systems)
- Lesson 4.5: The 100-10-3-1 Rule (Analyze 100, visit 10, offer on 3, close 1)
- **Worksheet**: Deal Source Tracker
- **Action Item**: Set up 3 active deal-sourcing channels

### Module 5: Analyzing Deals — The Numbers That Matter
- Lesson 5.1: The Rental Property Analysis Framework
- Lesson 5.2: Cash Flow Calculation (Gross rent - vacancy - expenses - debt service = cash flow)
- Lesson 5.3: Key Metrics Explained (Cap rate, cash-on-cash return, DSCR, GRM, ROI)
- Lesson 5.4: The 1% Rule, 50% Rule, and 70% Rule — When to Use Them
- Lesson 5.5: Running Comps and Estimating ARV
- Lesson 5.6: Estimating Rehab Costs (Room-by-room budgeting)
- Lesson 5.7: Walk-Through of a Full Deal Analysis (Live spreadsheet demo)
- **Worksheet**: Rental Property Analyzer Spreadsheet (fillable)
- **Worksheet**: Fix-and-Flip Calculator Spreadsheet (fillable)
- **Action Item**: Analyze 5 real properties using the provided spreadsheets

### Module 6: Financing Your Deals
- Lesson 6.1: Conventional Mortgages for Investors (20–25% down, DTI requirements, rate types)
- Lesson 6.2: FHA & House Hacking (3.5% down, owner-occupancy requirement, 1-year rule)
- Lesson 6.3: Hard Money & Private Money Lending
- Lesson 6.4: Seller Financing & Subject-To Deals
- Lesson 6.5: Home Equity (HELOC/Cash-Out Refi) to Fund Deals
- Lesson 6.6: Partnerships & Joint Ventures
- Lesson 6.7: Building Relationships with Lenders (What they look for, how to present yourself)
- **Worksheet**: Financing Options Comparison Matrix
- **Action Item**: Get pre-approved or establish a line of credit

### Module 7: Making Offers, Negotiation & Closing
- Lesson 7.1: Writing Strong Offers (Contingencies, earnest money, timelines)
- Lesson 7.2: Negotiation Tactics for Investors (Anchoring, motivated seller signals, win-win framing)
- Lesson 7.3: The Due Diligence Period (Inspections, title search, insurance, appraisal)
- Lesson 7.4: Understanding the Closing Process (Title company, escrow, closing costs)
- Lesson 7.5: Common Deal Killers and How to Avoid Them
- **Worksheet**: Offer Letter Template + Negotiation Script
- **Action Item**: Submit your first offer on a real property

### Module 8: Property Management & Building Your Portfolio
- Lesson 8.1: Self-Managing vs. Hiring a Property Manager
- Lesson 8.2: Tenant Screening (Credit, background, income verification, landlord references)
- Lesson 8.3: Lease Agreements — Key Clauses to Include
- Lesson 8.4: Maintenance Systems (Preventive schedules, vendor relationships, emergency protocols)
- Lesson 8.5: Bookkeeping & Tax Basics for Landlords
- Lesson 8.6: Planning Your Next Deal (Reinvesting cash flow, scaling the portfolio)
- **Worksheet**: Tenant Screening Checklist + Lease Template
- **Worksheet**: Monthly Property P&L Tracker
- **Action Item**: Create your property management system (even before you buy)

### BONUS Materials
- Bonus 1: Real Deal Breakdown — Full Walkthrough of an Actual Purchase
- Bonus 2: Rolodex of Recommended Tools, Software, and Service Providers
- Bonus 3: Investor Legal Entities — LLC, Land Trust, and Asset Protection Basics
- Bonus 4: 30-Day Quick-Start Action Plan

## COURSE 2: "Portfolio Accelerator" (Intermediate)

**Price point**: $997–$1,997
**Format**: Self-paced video + live group coaching calls (bi-weekly) + community
**Length**: 10 modules, ~35 hours of video content
**Prerequisite**: Owns at least 1 investment property
**Outcome**: Student scales from 1–5 properties to 10–50+ units using advanced strategies

### Module 1: From Investor to Business Owner
- Building your investing business (entity structure, banking, bookkeeping)
- Creating standard operating procedures for every repeatable task
- Hiring and managing a team (VAs, property managers, contractors, agents)
- Systems and automation tools

### Module 2: Advanced Market Analysis
- Demographic-driven demand forecasting (PwC/ULI 2026 framework)
- Employment concentration analysis and wage growth mapping
- Supply pipeline analysis (permits, construction starts, absorption rates)
- Identifying markets before they become "hot"

### Module 3: The BRRRR Strategy — Mastered
- Finding deep-value properties (70% of ARV target)
- Managing rehabs at scale (contractor SOPs, draw schedules, scope of work templates)
- Seasoning requirements and refinance timing
- Recycling capital — the infinite return strategy
- Case study: Full BRRRR deal from acquisition to refinance

### Module 4: Small Multifamily Investing (5–50 Units)
- Transitioning from residential to commercial valuation (income approach)
- Underwriting apartment deals (T-12 analysis, rent rolls, expense ratios)
- Value-add strategies (rent increases, expense reduction, capital improvements)
- Commercial financing (agency debt, CMBS, bank loans, credit unions)

### Module 5: Short-Term Rental Optimization
- Market selection for STR (regulation risk, demand drivers, seasonality)
- Listing optimization (photography, copywriting, pricing algorithms)
- Operations and automation (smart locks, dynamic pricing, cleaning teams)
- Financial modeling for STR (higher gross, higher expenses, net comparison)

### Module 6: Advanced Financing & Capital Stacking
- Portfolio loans and blanket mortgages
- Private money lending — raising capital from individuals
- Syndication basics — pooling investor capital
- Seller financing at scale
- The capital stack explained (senior debt, mezzanine, preferred equity, common equity)

### Module 7: Tax Strategy & Asset Protection
- Cost segregation studies — accelerated depreciation
- 1031 exchanges — rules, timelines, qualified intermediaries
- Real estate professional status (REPS) — the ultimate tax strategy
- Entity structuring for asset protection (LLC per property, series LLC, land trusts)
- Working with a RE-specialized CPA

### Module 8: Building & Managing a Property Management Operation
- In-house vs. third-party PM — the breakeven analysis
- PM software stack (AppFolio, Buildium, RentManager)
- Tenant relations, lease enforcement, and eviction process
- Maintenance and CapEx budgeting at scale

### Module 9: Portfolio Optimization & Performance Tracking
- Portfolio-level KPIs (NOI growth, equity growth, cash-on-cash, IRR)
- When to sell, refinance, or hold
- 1031 exchange strategies for portfolio rebalancing
- Building your personal financial dashboard

### Module 10: Building Your Investor Network
- Finding and vetting partners
- Investor meetups and masterminds
- Raising private capital (legally and ethically)
- Building referral relationships (agents, lenders, attorneys, CPAs)

## COURSE 3: "Capital Raiser" (Advanced)

**Price point**: $2,997–$5,997
**Format**: Cohort-based (8-week live program) + 1-on-1 coaching + community
**Length**: 8 live sessions (2 hrs each) + supplemental videos + office hours
**Prerequisite**: 5+ deals completed, ready to scale through OPM (Other People's Money)
**Outcome**: Student structures, raises capital for, and manages their first syndication or fund

### Module 1: The Syndication Business Model
- GP/LP structure explained
- Fee structures (acquisition, asset management, disposition, promotes)
- Alignment of interests and fiduciary duty
- Career paths: Sponsor, operator, capital raiser, hybrid

### Module 2: Securities Law & Compliance
- Regulation D — 506(b) vs. 506(c) offerings
- Accredited vs. sophisticated investors
- The PPM (Private Placement Memorandum), Operating Agreement, and Subscription Agreement
- Working with a securities attorney
- Blue sky filings and Form D

### Module 3: Underwriting Syndication Deals
- Conservative underwriting standards
- Sensitivity analysis and stress testing
- Waterfall structures and return modeling
- Investor return metrics (IRR, equity multiple, preferred return, cash-on-cash)

### Module 4: Building Your Investor Database
- Creating thought leadership content
- Hosting investor webinars and meetups
- CRM setup and investor relationship management
- The "warm list" strategy — converting your network
- Compliance: What you can/cannot say before and during a raise

### Module 5: Capital Raising Execution
- Creating the investor presentation deck
- The investor call framework (discovery, education, commitment)
- Overcoming common investor objections
- Soft commitments vs. hard commitments
- Wire instructions and subscription document flow

### Module 6: Asset Management & Investor Relations
- Monthly/quarterly reporting templates
- Distribution calculations and waterfall execution
- Property-level business plan execution
- Capital calls and refinance events
- Managing underperformance — communication strategies

### Module 7: Scaling the Syndication Business
- Building a team (asset manager, investor relations, acquisitions)
- Multiple deal structures (JV, syndication, fund)
- The fund model — 506(c) fund vs. deal-by-deal
- Track record building and institutional readiness

### Module 8: Exit Strategies & Legacy Building
- Disposition timing and strategy
- 1031 exchange at the fund level
- Building a brand that attracts deal flow and capital
- Long-term vision: From syndicator to fund manager to family office

## Cross-Cutting Curriculum Elements

These elements should appear across all three courses:

### Live Components
- Weekly or bi-weekly live Q&A calls (recorded for replay)
- Monthly deal review sessions (students submit deals for analysis)
- Guest expert interviews (attorneys, CPAs, lenders, experienced investors)

### Community Features
- Private discussion forum (Skool, Circle, or Discord)
- Accountability pods (groups of 4–6 students)
- Deal-sharing board
- Resource and vendor directory

### Assessment & Certification
- Module quizzes (knowledge checks)
- Deal analysis assignments (practical application)
- Final capstone: Present a complete deal analysis or business plan
- Certificate of completion (digital badge)
