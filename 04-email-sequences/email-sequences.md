## Sequence 2: Weekly Newsletter Template

Sent weekly to your entire list. Keeps you top-of-mind between launches.

---

**Subject line formulas** (rotate these):
- "How I [specific result] with [strategy]"
- "The [number] rule I follow before every deal"
- "[Myth] is costing you money — here's why"
- "I analyzed [X] deals this week — here's what I found"
- "[Market/city] update: what investors need to know"

**Newsletter Structure:**

```
1. HOOK (2–3 sentences)
   Start with a story, surprising fact, or contrarian opinion.

2. LESSON (Main body — 200–400 words)
   One clear idea, framework, or insight.
   Use short paragraphs (1–3 sentences each).
   Include a specific example or number.

3. ACTION ITEM (1–2 sentences)
   Tell them exactly what to do with this information.

4. CTA (1 sentence)
   Drive to YouTube video, blog post, webinar, or course.

5. SIGN-OFF
   Personal, consistent — build a recognizable voice.
```
