# Real Estate Investing Education Business — Content & Systems Blueprint

A complete, systematized framework for building and scaling an online real estate
investing education business. This repository contains curriculum designs, sales
funnel blueprints, email sequences, content calendars, and standard operating
procedures for autonomous content production.

## Repository Structure

```
.
├── 01-content-strategy/         # Market research, positioning, audience targeting
│   └── content-strategy.md
├── 02-course-curriculum/        # Full course outlines with module breakdowns
│   └── course-curriculum.md
├── 03-sales-funnel/             # Lead generation, nurture, and conversion systems
│   └── sales-funnel-blueprint.md
├── 04-email-sequences/          # Automated email nurture campaigns
│   └── email-sequences.md
├── 05-content-production/       # SOPs for systematic, autonomous content creation
│   └── content-production-system.md
└── README.md
```

## How to Use This Blueprint

1. **Start with the Content Strategy** — understand the market demand and your positioning.
2. **Review the Course Curriculum** — pick the tier (beginner, intermediate, advanced) to launch first.
3. **Set Up the Sales Funnel** — follow the step-by-step funnel blueprint.
4. **Deploy Email Sequences** — plug these into your email platform (ConvertKit, ActiveCampaign, etc.).
5. **Follow the Content Production SOPs** — build a repeatable weekly content engine.

Each document is designed to be actionable on its own and part of the larger system.
