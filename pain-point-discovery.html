📋 The spreadsheet test
If people track something in a spreadsheet that they wish was automated — that's a product. Ask: "What do you do manually that drives you crazy?"
💼 The job ad test
Companies hiring humans for repetitive tasks = automation opportunity. Search for "data entry," "manual tracking," "spreadsheet coordinator" in any industry.
⭐ Software review mining
Filter G2/Capterra for 2–3 star reviews. People say exactly what's missing. "Great but doesn't have X" = your next product idea.
